## Why upgrade? Older versions don't receive fixes for changes in browsers and on video sites, and bundle older Java runtimes. XDM 8 and the upcoming XDM 9 fix hundreds of issues and are faster at both regular and video downloads. ## Moving to the current version Install the current version over the old one; see [Update & migrate](/guide/update-and-migrate/). Your old download list isn't imported, so finish or note pending downloads first.
