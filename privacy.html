This policy explains what data is collected when you visit xtremedownloadmanager.com (“the website”), and how the Xtreme Download Manager application (“XDM”) and its browser extension (“XDM Integration Module”) handle your data.

Summary

  • The XDM app and browser extension don't send your data to the developer or any third party. The extension talks only to the XDM app on your own computer.
  • The website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and Google AdSense to show ads. Both use cookies. You can control them as described below.

1. This website

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), your browser and device, and how you arrived at the site. This data is aggregated. We don't use it to identify you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The website shows ads served by Google AdSense, which keeps the website and the free XDM software running.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads and related cookies are used, through a consent message provided by Google.

Cookies on this website

Apart from analytics and advertising cookies, the website stores one preference in your browser's local storage: your choice of light or dark theme. You can clear it any time through your browser settings.

Links to other sites

The website links to GitHub, browser extension stores and other sites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

2. The XDM application

XDM does not collect personal information and contains no spyware, analytics or advertising components. Download details (addresses, file names, cookies and headers needed to resume a download) are stored only on your computer, in the .xdm-app folder of your home directory, until you delete the download.

XDM connects to the internet to download the files you ask for, sending requests only to the servers those files come from, and to check for new versions using the public GitHub releases API.

3. The XDM browser extension

When browser monitoring is on, the extension reads the information needed to hand a download or video to XDM: the download's web address, the page it came from, request and response headers, and the site's cookies. This data is sent only to the XDM application on your own computer at http://127.0.0.1:8597, an address that never leaves your machine. The extension has no server and sends nothing to the developer or any third party. It does not read form contents, passwords, keystrokes or page text. On YouTube it captures nothing.

The full extension policy is published with its source code on GitHub.

4. Children

The website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new “Updated” date.
