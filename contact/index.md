## Need help using XDM?

1. Check the [help center](/help/) and the [FAQ](/faq/). Most questions are answered there.
2. Ask on the **[XDM community forum](https://github.com/subhra74/xdm/discussions)** (GitHub Discussions).

## Found a bug?

Open an issue on the **[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/subhra74/xdm/issues)**. Please include:

- your XDM version and operating system,
- the browser and extension version (for capture problems),
- the exact error message and steps to reproduce,
- log files if relevant (see [internal error](/help/errors/internal-error/) for where to find them).

## Other enquiries

For press, partnerships, privacy questions or anything else, reach out via
[X / Twitter (@XDM_subhra74)](https://twitter.com/XDM_subhra74) or open a discussion on GitHub.
