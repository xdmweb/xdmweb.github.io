## Brave tips

- Brave shows a short warning that the extension isn't reviewed by Brave. Click <span class="ui">Add extension</span> to continue.
- **Shields** don't block the extension, but strict fingerprinting protection can stop some video players from
  loading. Lower Shields for that site if a video isn't detected.
- **Private windows**: open `brave://extensions` → *Details* → *Allow in Private*.
