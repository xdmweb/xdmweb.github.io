## Edge tips

- **“Allow extensions from other stores”**: the first time you install from the Chrome Web Store, Edge shows a
  banner at the top. Click <span class="ui">Allow extensions from other stores</span>, then <span class="ui">Add to Chrome</span>
  (it installs into Edge).
- **Pin it**: click the puzzle-piece icon → the eye icon next to *XDM Integration Module*.
- **Edge's own download bar** may still flash briefly before XDM takes over. That's normal.
