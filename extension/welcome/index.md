## Finish setup

<ol class="steps-list">
  <li><strong>Pin the XDM icon.</strong> Click the puzzle-piece icon in the browser toolbar and pin
    <em>XDM Integration Module</em>. Videos the extension finds appear there, with a counter on the icon.</li>
  <li><strong>Make sure the XDM app is running.</strong> The extension hands everything to the XDM app on your computer.
    Don't have it yet? <a href="/download/">Download XDM</a>, free for Windows, macOS and Linux.</li>
  <li><strong>Try it.</strong> Click a download link for a ZIP or installer on any site. XDM's <em>New download</em>
    window appears instead of the browser's download bar.</li>
</ol>

If the popup says **XDM isn't running**, start the app. If it says **Site access is restricted**, click
**Allow on all sites**. More fixes: [extension can't connect](/help/extension-cannot-connect/).


## What you can do now

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/img/shots/video-popup.webp" alt="XDM extension popup listing videos found on a page" width="650" height="650" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>Play a video and pick a format from the XDM popup.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Save the video you're watching**: play it, click the XDM icon and pick a quality.
  [How to download videos](/guide/download-streaming-video/).
- **Grab every link on a page**: right-click → *Download all with XDM…*, or *Grab all links for this page…* in the popup.
  [Batch download](/guide/batch-download/).
- **Choose what XDM captures**: file types and blocked sites are in XDM → Settings → Browser.
- **Download faster and resume anything**: [Speed up downloads](/guide/speed-up-downloads/) and
  [Refresh link](/guide/refresh-link/).


## Your privacy

The extension sends download details only to the XDM app on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`). It has no server,
no analytics and no tracking, and it stays off YouTube. See the [privacy policy](/privacy.html).
