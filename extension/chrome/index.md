## Chrome tips

- **Managed by your organisation?** Work or school Chrome profiles can block extensions. Ask your administrator.
- **Several Chrome profiles** need the extension in each profile.
- **Incognito**: open `chrome://extensions`, click *Details* on XDM and enable *Allow in Incognito* if you want it there.
